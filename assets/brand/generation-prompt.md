# Fathom 第二轮：层叠深潭

工具：内置 image_gen。

参考：已查看 Folia、FaroPDF、Funes、Badminton Lab 的实际应用图标。生成时使用 FaroPDF 与 Funes 的图标作风格参考，不复制主体。该稿探索由线条标记转为有层次的实体图形，属于待确认新方向，不修改项目现行设计合同与图标资产。

## 提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE new distinctive premium macOS application icon for Fathom, a local storage history and directory exploration app. Name evokes depth sounding and understanding. This is a fresh visual concept, not an edit of the reference logos.
Input image 1 (FaroPDF): reference ONLY for crafted dimensional paper-layer elegance, ivory macOS tile and tactile restrained shadow. DO NOT copy lighthouse, documents or warm light.
Input image 2 (Funes): reference ONLY for one strong memorable organic silhouette and readable blue/white massing. DO NOT copy feather, leaf, branches or circuit nodes.
New Fathom visual subject: a sculptural bathymetric basin, a deep pool seen from slightly above, composed of exactly four broad nested softly asymmetric rounded triangular/pebble-shaped contour terraces that descend toward a dark navy central hollow. A distinctive smooth rounded teardrop basin silhouette leaning subtly diagonally, with each nested edge gently offset toward lower right to show depth. NOT concentric perfect circles and NOT fingerprint lines. The shapes must feel like a beautifully designed abstract topographical object, not a landscape illustration. Main silhouette compact and bold, generous broad solid surfaces, no spindly outlines, no unnecessary detail. The eye reads DEPTH instantly. One tiny ivory measuring notch at top edge may indicate sounding, integrated into the layer; no separate pointer, stick, magnifier or ring.
Palette: muted trench blue #345D7F and mineral teal #3E7E7C, light mist blue outer lip, deeper blue/teal terraces toward midnight navy hollow. Solid color surfaces with only quiet natural material shading at the step edges, not decorative gradients. Matte ceramic or carefully layered thick paper feeling, refined crafted depth, never glossy plastic.
Composition: square 1024x1024 finished icon presentation. One large ivory-white macOS rounded-square tile, centered, about 86% of canvas width with precise generous corner radius; the distinctive basin symbol fills about 65% of tile. A light warm-gray fully opaque background behind the tile, no alpha or transparency in this presentation. Very subtle soft contact shadow under tile and under contour step edges. Beautiful visual balance, strong thumbnail recognition, clean craftsmanship. Absolutely NO text, no wordmark, no comparison board, no captions, no UI, no other objects.
Avoid: power button, smile face, stopwatch, radar rings, wireframe outlines, target, eyes, feather, leaf, paper documents, lighthouse, literal waves, anchors, compasses, charts, hard drive, neon glow, glass, chrome, oversaturated cyan/purple, gaudy 3D, intricate miniature landscape. Prioritize one memorable sculptural basin emblem that can later be simplified to a flat silhouette.
